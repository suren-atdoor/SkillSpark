# Quiz Master — User Guide

Welcome to Quiz Master. This guide helps you sign in, take quizzes, save progress, and review results.

What you can do
- Take quizzes with multiple question types: multiple choice, multiple select, true/false, fill-in-the-blank, short answer, essay, matching, and ordering
- Save your progress automatically (when signed in) or manually
- Resume where you left off
- View your results and detailed explanations
- See your saved attempts and statistics in your Dashboard

1) Getting started
- Open the app. On the Home page:
  - Sign Up to create a new account or Sign In to resume progress.
  - Or start a quiz without an account (progress won’t be saved across sessions).

2) Creating an account or signing in
- Click Sign Up or Sign In.
- Enter your details and submit.
- If successful, you’ll be redirected to your Dashboard with access to saved progress.

Tip: Signed-in users get auto-save while taking a quiz, a Dashboard to continue saved attempts, and history.

3) Taking a quiz
- From Home, choose “Take Quiz.”
- You’ll see:
  - Title and timer (if set by the quiz)
  - Question counter and progress bar
  - One question at a time with the appropriate input controls
- Navigate:
  - Use Next, Previous or the “Question Navigation” buttons at the bottom to jump to a specific question.
- Submit:
  - Click “Submit Quiz” on the last question, or when you’re done.

4) Saving and resuming
- Auto-save:
  - If you’re signed in, your answers and time spent are auto-saved every ~30 seconds and when you click “Save Now.”
- Manual save:
  - Click “Save Now” in the green banner at the top while taking a quiz.
- Resume:
  - Go to Dashboard → “Continue” on any in-progress quiz to resume exactly where you left off.

5) Results and explanations
- After submitting, your Results page shows:
  - Score, grade, total points, time spent
  - Pass/Fail (if the quiz has a required passing score)
  - Per-question review:
    - Your answer vs. correct answer
    - Explanation (if provided)
- You can print your results or retake the quiz.

6) Security features during a quiz
- Some quizzes can restrict copy/paste or screenshots:
  - You may see warning messages if restricted actions are detected.
  - Repeated violations could end the exam automatically depending on quiz settings.
- These settings are visible in the security status banner within the quiz.

7) Dashboard
- Access recently saved progress, quickly resume, and see basic stats like quizzes completed, average score, and time spent.
- “Start New Quiz” to begin another quiz anytime.

8) Accessibility
- Keyboard navigation supported for inputs and buttons.
- High contrast and readable fonts; works on mobile, tablet, and desktop.

9) Troubleshooting
- I can’t see any questions:
  - Try refreshing the page. If the problem persists, return to Home and click “Take Quiz” again.
- I’m signed in but my progress didn’t save:
  - Ensure your browser isn’t blocking local storage or network requests.
  - Try the “Save Now” button during the quiz.
- I forgot my login details:
  - Use your standard Sign In info. If you need help, contact the app owner/administrator.

Data and privacy
- Your saved progress and quiz attempts are associated with your account and visible only to you (and administrators, if configured).
- If deployed on Vercel with Git, changes are continuously deployed from your repository; environment variables are configured in the project settings on Vercel [^2][^3][^4][^5].

Happy learning!
